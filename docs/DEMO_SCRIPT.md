# 2-minute product demo script

## Opening (15 seconds)

“I researched CloudEagle's public AI governance and non-human identity workflows and built a small independent concept prototype around one operational problem: what happens when an employee leaves but the AI agent they created remains active?”

## Step 1 — Establish the problem (25 seconds)

“On the Overview, you can see a small fictional enterprise inventory. The queue highlights departed owners, excessive permissions, and dormant identities. These are separate signals because they require different decisions.”

Open **Agent inventory**, select **Deal Desk Copilot**.

## Step 2 — Explain the context (30 seconds)

“This agent's owner has departed, but the agent remains active. It has Salesforce admin access and can reach contract data. Instead of showing only a risk score, the profile exposes the owner, discovery source, credential type, last activity, permissions, and connected resources so an administrator can understand why it was flagged.”

## Step 3 — Demonstrate safe remediation (35 seconds)

“Now I can plan the response. I can reassign ownership, remove admin access, rotate the credential, or suspend the agent. The admin must choose the steps, and the new owner is explicitly selected. In local demo mode, these actions update browser-saved sample data. When signed in through Supabase, the fictional workspace state persists to the user’s isolated database record; no provider-specific integrations are called.”

Select **Reassign owner** and **Remove admin access**, then record the plan.

## Step 4 — Show operational evidence (20 seconds)

“The action appears in the Audit Trail. Review completion is a separate workflow and does not automatically change permissions. The offboarding scenario adds a new risk and review assignment while leaving the identity active until an administrator decides what to do.”

## Close (10 seconds)

“The product question I wanted to explore is how to reduce the time from risk discovery to an explainable, accountable decision, while protecting business continuity. I’ve included the research, PRD, acceptance criteria, and QA plan with the working prototype.”

## Reset before sharing

Open **Demo Guide → Reset demo data** to restore the initial fictional data. This is useful before each interview or review.
